# Notice

*UNATCO Continues* is an unofficial fan project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by
the owners of Deus Ex or by the makers of Deus Ex: Revision.

## What the licence covers

The [MIT licence](LICENSE) covers the original work in this repository: the mod's
UnrealScript, the tools and scripts, the documentation, and the dialogue written for the
mod.

## What it does not cover

- **Deus Ex and Deus Ex: Revision.** No game files are included: the maps are patches applied
  to your own copy of Deus Ex, and the mod needs the original game to run. Free, not for sale.
  Names, characters and setting belong to their owners.
- **The map patches (`maps/patches`, `maps/vanilla/patches`).** They hold the mod's changes to maps
  of the game and are made into maps only from your own copy of it. What they change is offered
  under the MIT licence; the game's maps are not.
- **`UCAnims.u`** (in the installers). A character model of the game with the mod's new
  animations, as mods for this game usually ship. It is not offered under the MIT licence.
- **`src/UnatcoContinues/Textures/UCSignElevators.*`.** A small sign redrawn in the style
  of the Revision texture it replaces. It is included so the mod builds; it is not offered
  under the MIT licence.
- **Voices.** No recording, sample or voice identifier is included. The voice tools in
  `tools/voices` need your own ElevenLabs account and key.
