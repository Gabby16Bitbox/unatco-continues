# UNATCO Route — Hong Kong e oltre (design di Gabby, 3 ott 2026)

Fonte d'ispirazione: design document v13.12 di Ion Storm (in una versione precedente UNATCO dava la caccia a Tracer Tong
e Maggie Chow voleva usare JC per condurre MJ12 da lui; Red Arrow sotto l'influenza di MJ12, Luminous Path legata
agli Illuminati). https://deusex.fandom.com/wiki/Deus_Ex_design_document_version_13.12

Struttura della campagna: branch originale -> Hong Kong/Tong -> VersaLife -> New York/UNATCO -> finale interno UNATCO/MJ12.

## MISSION 1 — HONG KONG: THE HUNT

Premessa: JC ha rifiutato Paul, non ha trasmesso il segnale. STATUS: ACTIVE, ORGANIZATION: UNATCO, KILLSWITCH: INACTIVE.
Ordine: LOCATE TRACER TONG — APPREHEND OR ELIMINATE. Paul e' un fuggitivo.

1. **Arrivo** (06_HongKong_Helibase al contrario: MJ12/security FRIENDLY, JC AUTHORIZED, elicottero CLEARED).
   - JOCK: These guys don't look like UNATCO. / JC: They're expecting us. / JOCK: That wasn't what I said.
   - Un ufficiale va incontro a JC: OFFICER: Agent Denton? / JC: Yes. / OFFICER: Welcome to Hong Kong. Mr. Simons requested
     that you contact him immediately. / JC: Majestic Twelve. / OFFICER: Special Projects Directorate. / JC: UNATCO? /
     OFFICER: Coalition. (Non si spiega altro; simboli MJ12 visti per la prima volta.)
2. **Briefing di Simons** (InfoLink): Denton. / I'm in Hong Kong. / Good. / Tracer Tong has provided technical assistance to
   terrorist organizations operating in several jurisdictions. / Including the NSF. / Including your brother. / (pausa) /
   What's his location? / Unknown. / But we have someone who can help you find him.
   Goal CONTACT MAGGIE CHOW: "Maggie Chow is a local VersaLife executive with contacts inside the Hong Kong Triads. Meet her
   at Queen's Tower."
3. **Primo indizio**: JC: Why is VersaLife involved? / SIMONS: VersaLife has extensive interests in Hong Kong. / JC: That
   doesn't answer the question. / SIMONS: It answers the part you need to know. (Informazione compartimentata.)
4. **Wan Chai**: stessa Hong Kong, relazioni cambiate. Red Arrow NEUTRAL/FRIENDLY (Maggie li manipola, MJ12 li influenza),
   Luminous Path SUSPICIOUS, Police FRIENDLY, VersaLife FRIENDLY.
5. **Max Chen** (prima di Maggie): Mr. Denton. / You know who I am. / Everyone knows who you are. / I'm looking for Tracer
   Tong. / (sorride) Then you are looking for the Luminous Path. / And Maggie Chow? / She is expecting you.
   (RED ARROW -> MAGGIE -> UNATCO/MJ12, senza spiegarlo.)
6. **Maggie Chow** (riscrivere MeetMaggie; sa che JC e' UNATCO): Mr. J.C. Denton. / Maggie Chow. / As serious as your brother.
   / You knew Paul? / Everyone involved with Tracer Tong knew Paul. / That's not what I asked. / (pausa) Yes.
   La sua versione: Tong ha radicalizzato Paul; Luminous Path protegge Tong; la guerra fra Triadi e' colpa del Luminous Path;
   Red Arrow cerca di mantenere l'ordine; Tong usa la guerra come copertura.
   MAGGIE: Your brother came here looking for answers. / JC: What did he find? / MAGGIE: Tracer Tong. / (pausa) Look what
   happened to him.
7. **Obiettivi**: Gordon Quick non fara' mai entrare un agente UNATCO: bisogna guadagnarne la fiducia.
   Primary LOCATE TRACER TONG; Secondary INVESTIGATE THE LUMINOUS PATH; Optional INVESTIGATE THE DEATH OF RED ARROW
   LEADER YUEN KONG.
8. **Gordon Quick** (ostile a parole, non attacca): Paul Denton trusted you. / Paul made a mistake. / Which one? / Choosing
   terrorists over UNATCO. / (sorride) No. Trusting his brother. / Where's Tong? / Somewhere you will never find him. / Then
   we're finished. / Maybe. / (JC se ne va) Ask Maggie Chow who killed Yuen Kong. (JC si ferma.)
9. **Indagine** (classico Deus Ex): seguire Maggie; indagare su cio' che dice Gordon; entrare nell'appartamento di Maggie;
   stazione di polizia; hackerare computer VersaLife / Red Arrow. Tutte le strade convergono: MAGGIE STA MENTENDO.
10. **Dragon's Tooth** nell'area privata di Maggie (come nel gioco originale), ma ora Maggie e' il contatto ufficiale
    indicato da Simons.
11. **JC contatta Simons**: I found the Dragon's Tooth. / Good. / In Maggie Chow's apartment. / (silenzio) And? / She told me
    the Luminous Path stole it. / Maggie Chow is a confidential asset. / She murdered the Red Arrow leader. / Can you prove
    that? / I can prove she's lying. / Then you have learned something useful. / You knew. / (pausa) Your objective is Tracer
    Tong. (Cuore della missione.)
12. **JC non abbandona UNATCO**: If Chow compromised the operation, she needs to be investigated. / SIMONS: After Tong is in
    custody. / JC: Understood. (corrupt asset != corrupt organization)
13. **Esporre Maggie** (prova a Max Chen): Maggie had this? / Yes. / Then she killed Yuen Kong. / That's what the evidence
    suggests. / And you're working with her. / I'm working for UNATCO. / Is there a difference? / (silenzio)
14. **Tregua delle Triadi**: Max ferma il conflitto. Gordon: Tong will speak with you. / JC: I didn't ask to speak with him. /
    GORDON: No. You came to arrest him.
15. **Laboratorio di Tong** (JC entra come nemico; nessuno estrae le armi): J.C. Denton. / Tracer Tong. / Paul said you
    would come. / Paul thought I'd come for help. / And instead? / You're coming with me. / (sorriso) To UNATCO. / That's
    right. / Even now. / Especially now.
16. **Prove, non discorsi**: Paul tried to convince you. / He didn't. / Because he gave you a conclusion. / (terminale)
    I'll give you evidence. Terminale: VERSALIFE / AMBROSIA / GRAY DEATH / NANOTECH RESEARCH (incompleto).
    Where did you get this? / Ask your employers. / I'm asking you. / VersaLife.
17. **Il trucco di Maggie**: impulso del communicator di JC; allarme. TONG: You led them here. / Who? / You still don't know?
18. **Assalto MJ12** (JC era il localizzatore): SIMONS: Denton. / There's a tactical team entering the compound. / Correct.
    / You tracked me. / We tracked Tong. / Through me. / You completed your assignment.
19. **Nuovo ordine** SECURE TRACER TONG: Stand by. The recovery team will take him into custody. / He's my prisoner. /
    Not anymore.
20. **Tong fugge** (nessuna scelta uccidi/salva): Come with me. / No. / Then look at what they do after I leave.
    MJ12 distrugge server, uccide tecnici, recupera materiale, piazza esplosivi.
    JC: Orders were to secure the facility. / MJ12 COMMANDER: Our orders are different.
21. **Prima scelta morale**: MJ12 sta per giustiziare un tecnico Luminous Path disarmato.
    Non intervenire -> UNATCORoute_Compliant++. Ordinare di fermarsi: He's unarmed. / Move along, Agent Denton. / That's
    an order. / Not from my chain of command. -> UNATCORoute_QuestionsMJ12++ (il comandante lascia il prigioniero ma
    ricorda).
22. **Tong sparito**: obiettivo TRACER TONG: ESCAPED; JC recupera VERSALIFE DATA FRAGMENT.
23. **Simons fuori dal compound**: Tong escaped. / Your team alerted him. / Our team prevented him from destroying valuable
    intelligence. / I had him. / You were talking to him. / I was interrogating him. / Then consider the interrogation over.
    / Tong gave me information about VersaLife. / (pausa) What information? / That's what I'm going to find out. / Denton...
    / You wanted Tong. / He led me to VersaLife. / I'm following the lead. / (pausa) Very well.
    END MISSION 1 — NEW PRIMARY GOAL: REPORT TO VERSALIFE (come agente autorizzato, non infiltrato).

## MISSION 2 — VERSALIFE
La porta principale si apre per JC. RECEPTIONIST: Welcome, Agent Denton. Accesso ufficiale: laboratori, ricercatori,
security, MJ12, tutti amici (all'inizio). Obiettivo ufficiale: investigate a security breach linked to Tracer Tong; recuperare
stolen nanotechnology research. In profondita': GRAY DEATH -> AMBROSIA -> UNIVERSAL CONSTRUCTOR -> MJ12: la Gray Death viene
fabbricata qui (Level 2 labs, Universal Constructor come nel gioco originale).
Differenza: JC si fida ancora dell'UNATCO -> trova la prova -> la manda ai superiori.
JC: Simons. / Go ahead. / They're manufacturing the Gray Death. / (silenzio) / Did you hear me? / SIMONS: Yes.
(Simons non e' sorpreso.)

## MISSION 3 — RETURN TO NEW YORK
Simons ordina il ritorno: DEBRIEFING (in realta' CONTAINMENT). UNATCO HQ ancora amica: Janice, Carter, Alex, Manderley.
ALEX: You weren't supposed to come back. / Manderley ordered the debriefing. / Manderley didn't order it. / (pausa) Simons did.
Ordini: LOCATE AND APPREHEND PAUL DENTON; DETENTION OF ALEX JACOBSON (accesso non autorizzato ai dati).
JC deve decidere cosa significa LOYALTY TO UNATCO.

## MISSION 4 — UNATCO (finale della mini-campagna)
Domanda finale: WHO DOES UNATCO BELONG TO? (UNATCO -> Simons -> Majestic 12 -> Bob Page). JC pensa ancora che l'idea di UNATCO
abbia valore.
- **Ending A — The Loyalist**: JC accetta; Paul catturato, Tong eliminato, Simons promuove JC. SIMONS: Your brother couldn't
  separate the institution from the people running it. / Maybe he didn't want to. / And you? / I follow orders. CUT TO BLACK.
- **Ending B — UNATCO**: JC rifiuta MJ12 senza unirsi all'NSF ("UNATCO is not Majestic 12"); combatte MJ12 dentro la sede,
  libera Alex/Carter, espone Simons. PAUL: You finally figured it out. / You were wrong. / About what? / UNATCO. / (pausa)
  It doesn't need to be destroyed. It needs to be taken back. CUT TO BLACK. DEUS EX: UNATCO.

Note: non distruggere subito Maggie (serve ancora); Simons sa che mente e la protegge come asset.
